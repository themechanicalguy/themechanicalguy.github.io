Built to Adapt

I graduated in Mechanical Engineering and started out in core mechanical work — but the motivation and long-term vision just weren't there. So I pivoted to software in three months, and never looked back.

I began at a four-person startup, trained in web development, and shipped across a wide range of tech — blockchain, Ethereum, D3.js and plenty more. That's where I found my edge: I can get productive in almost any technology within about 15 days.

Today I work across the full stack with a frontend focus: React, Next.js & TypeScript on top, Node.js, APIs and databases underneath — testing baked in from day one, because engineering without verification is just a guess.

I also build AI-assisted workflows into my day-to-day engineering and ship LLM-powered features — streaming chat UIs, RAG-backed search, MCP integrations — with a focus on verifying and testing AI output, not just accepting it.
