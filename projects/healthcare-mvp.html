Built MVP for Health Care Startup

I served as the lead developer for the startup’s initial digital infrastructure, building their core platform from the ground up to support the company’s launch digitally.

ROLELead Developer
DOMAINHealthcare
SCOPEMVP · From scratch

Overview

An early-stage healthcare startup needed a digital foundation before launch — not a prototype throwaway, but a core platform the company could actually run on. As lead developer / Architect, I owned that build end to end: architecture, implementation, and getting the product ready for real users.

What I Built

  • Core application infrastructure to support the company’s digital launch.
  • Product surfaces and workflows needed for the initial MVP release.
  • A maintainable full-stack foundation the team could extend after launch.

Approach

Started from zero and shaped the platform around launch-critical flows — keeping the stack lean, the architecture clear, and delivery focused on what the business needed to go live.

Worked closely with stakeholders to translate early product requirements into a shippable MVP, balancing speed with enough structure to avoid rewriting everything later.
