Codession — Full-Stack Launchpad

Trainee in a six-person startup — the jump from mechanical engineering into software. Eleven months, three live products, different stacks, client-facing work from early on.

Overview

Codession was a small, fast shop. Training was the job: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, jQuery, PHP, MySQL, WordPress, Angular — then onto client work, not a sandbox. Three products in eleven months, each a different domain. That stretch is where “I can pick up a stack and finish the thing” stopped being a slogan.

Real estate listing

A property listing site that needed to work on phones and across browsers. I tightened the frontend, chased UI/UX defects, and improved load behaviour so mobile sessions did not bounce. Iterative build-and-test was the whole lifecycle, not a handoff at the end.

Web3 dApp

A decentralized app on Ethereum: Solidity smart contracts, AngularJS on the client, D3.js for live charts, IPFS for files. Contracts were written and tested in Remix. Early blockchain work — enough to ship against a moving ecosystem, not a tutorial chain.

E-learning (LAMP + WordPress)

An online learning product on WordPress and the LAMP stack. I helped build and customise it — plugins and features so educators could publish and learners could get through content without fighting the CMS.

Approach

Six people, high ownership, no spare bench. You learned the stack for the next client, then you were on the ticket. Client-facing work started while the training was still running. Production code, not classroom exercises — and the habit of switching verticals without waiting for a specialist.
